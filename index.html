Education

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Harvard University (2018–2023), advised by Prof. Milind Tambe, with a thesis on actualizing the impact of AI and optimization in public health. I also hold a B.Tech and M.Tech (integrated dual degree) in Electrical Engineering, with a minor in Computer Science, from the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay (2013–2018).

Research

I'm excited about building reliable AI systems for decision-making under uncertainty. My Ph.D. research applied ideas from restless multi-armed bandits, learning, and causal inference to public health challenges. Today, at Microsoft, my focus has grown to include LLM reasoning agents, evaluation and safety, cost-aware LLM routing, and retrieval-augmented reasoning (RAG / GraphRAG).

At Microsoft, I work on the AI Model & Solution team within Azure, developing probabilistic models and reasoning agents to improve the quality and reliability of Azure services, and translating research into deployed ML systems in product settings.

During my Ph.D., I built novel algorithms for solving fundamental computational challenges in real-world contexts such as tuberculosis prevention and improving maternal & child health. Transcending traditional research boundaries, I've strived to deploy my research in the real world — as of early 2023, it has helped support more than 100,000 mothers enrolled through our partner NGO.
